Quick 3-Step Setup Guide

1

Download & Run

Click "Download Desktop POS Launcher" or "Silent Print Agent" above. Once downloaded, double-click to launch.

2

Plug In USB/LAN Printer

Connect your thermal receipt printer via USB or Wi-Fi/LAN. FoodyPay will automatically scan and detect the printer name.

3

Print Instantly (No Popups!)

Click "Print Bill" or "Send to KOT" in FoodyPay. The receipt prints immediately without opening any print dialog box!
